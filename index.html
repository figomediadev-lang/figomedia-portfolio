( 08 ) Behind the Frame & FAQ
▶ Play Showreel 2026

From the other side of the lens — a 60-second compilation of recent commercial, automotive, real estate, and lifestyle productions across the UAE.

What kind of video projects do you take on?

+

We produce social reels, automotive and motorsport films, luxury real estate villa tours, commercial product shoots, and paid ad creatives for brands wanting a premium aesthetic coupled with algorithmic conversion performance.

How do you deliver aspect ratios (16:9 vs 9:16)?

+

We shoot specifically with multi-framing guidelines. Every campaign is delivered in native 9:16 vertical for TikTok and Instagram Reels, 16:9 widescreen for website heroes, YouTube, and broadcast, plus 1:1 square for feed carousels.

Do we need a massive crew or huge budget?

+

No. Our methodology is built on minimal, clever setups — practical lighting, motors, smoke, and cinema cameras. The crew only scales when the story and scale demand it, keeping production nimble and cost-effective.

Do you manage food and retail delivery aggregators too?

+

Yes. Figo Media manages end-to-end operations across Talabat, Noon, Deliveroo, Careem, Smiles, and Keeta — including menu optimization, dynamic pricing, in-app ad bidding, and financial reconciliation.
