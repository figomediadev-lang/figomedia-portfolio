( 08 ) Behind the Frame & FAQ
▶ Play Cinema Reel

From the other side of the lens — a high-energy compilation of recent festival, automotive, corporate, and lifestyle productions across Dubai & the UAE.

What kind of video projects do you produce?

+

We produce high-impact social reels, motorsport & automotive films, music festival & live concert coverage, corporate summit documentaries, luxury real estate tours, and multi-channel paid ad creative for brands seeking a luxury aesthetic paired with algorithmic conversion.

How do you deliver aspect ratios (16:9 vs 9:16)?

+

We shoot specifically with multi-framing guidelines. Every campaign is cut in native 9:16 vertical for TikTok and Instagram Reels, 16:9 widescreen for websites, YouTube, and broadcast, plus 1:1 square for feed carousels.

Do we need a massive crew or huge budget?

+

No. Our methodology is built on minimal, clever setups — practical lighting, camera rigs, and high-velocity pacing. The crew only scales when the story and scale demand it, keeping productions agile, cost-effective, and fast.

Do you manage food and retail delivery aggregators too?

+

Yes. Figo Media manages end-to-end operations across Talabat, Noon, Deliveroo, Careem, Smiles, and Keeta — including menu optimization, dynamic pricing, in-app ad bidding, and financial reconciliation.
